STRAWBERRY CREEK VOICES Where Did You Last Hear It? A pocket score for one listener or two Stand somewhere you can linger. An open window will do. Give the place three minutes. SURFACE WATER Choose a sound that comes and goes. A tire on wet pavement will do, or the little slap a loose board makes under one shoe. Point to where you first heard it. Keep listening. Point to where you last heard it. How far did your hand have to move? UNDERFLOW Listen through the gap it leaves. Sometimes a quieter sound has been there the whole time. Give it the plainest name you can. If all you can say is "something ticking," keep those words. Let the next loud sound pass. Can you pick up the tick again? DEEP SILENCE Find the spot where the sound ran out for you. Maybe the tire went round a corner. Maybe a bus came between you and the sound. Stand there a little longer in your mind. The rider has gone on with an afternoon of their own. WITH A SECOND LISTENER Before you talk, each point to where you last heard the sound. If your hands point different ways, hold them there a moment. Tell each other what you heard in the last second. Two people at the same window may have followed different wheels. TAKE ONE LINE HOME "I could hear __________ as far as __________." One listener's line might read: "I could hear the skateboard as far as the change from brick to asphalt. After that I could still see the boy's red sleeve." Try the same place another day. Keep both lines. Read Strawberry Creek Voices https://remfan1994.github.io/strawberry-creek-voices/
